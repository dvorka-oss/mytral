How to obtain your Polar Flow (AccessLink) API credentials:

  1. Sign in at admin.polaraccesslink.com with your normal Polar Flow account.
  2. Click Create client. Accept the AccessLink Limited License Agreement and fill in the application information.
  3. Set the OAuth2 authorization callback URL to your MyTraL host, e.g. http://127.0.0.1:5000/polar/auth-callback for local use.
  4. Copy the Client ID and Client Secret.
  5. Paste both values into the form below and click Save Secrets.

Your secrets are stored encrypted on disk and are never displayed again after saving.
